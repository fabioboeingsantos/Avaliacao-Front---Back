# Prompt da arte da Flora do Vale

Ferramenta: geração integrada de imagens (skill imagegen), com fundo transparente.
Arquivo final: `frontend/public/marca/flora-do-vale-nucleo-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production-ready transparent PNG artwork for Flora do Vale, a Brazilian community-garden AI assistant.
Primary request: create one original botanical intelligence core that will float inside animated circular interface rings. The existing Cesta do Vale images are richly detailed photorealistic cutouts of wicker baskets and fresh vegetables, with warm studio lighting, realistic leaf veins, natural material textures and clean transparent silhouettes; match that level of realism and warmth.
Subject: a single small luminous seed-like pearl, warm cream and harvest-gold at its center, cradled by three elegant fresh garden-green leaves. The leaves curl upward around the pearl to form a round, balanced organic orb. Real leaf veins, subtle translucent edges, a few delicate golden pollen-like specks close to the core. Friendly, calm, refined.
Composition: centered object occupying about 75% of a square canvas, generous clean transparent margins, complete silhouette, frontal slightly three-quarter view, balanced at small display size.
Palette: garden green #2F6B3B, deep green #1F4D2E, subtle cream #F7F5EF, harvest gold #E6A23C, with very restrained warm copper highlights.
Lighting: soft photographic studio lighting and a restrained warm internal glow. Crisp botanical details and believable materials.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background and transparent corners; only the botanical core, no floor, no scene, no text, no letters, no labels, no logo, no watermark. Do not draw surrounding HUD rings, interface panels or a UI mockup; the website will animate the surrounding rings separately. No human face, no humanoid robot, no cartoon mascot. Original identity for Flora do Vale.
```
